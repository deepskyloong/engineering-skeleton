# W08c architecture-admission map

- Date: 2026-11-20
- Source: bun-toolkit `4c343e7`, gearset `3029152`, python-toolkit `739cd70`, personal-agent-kit `ab85ddb` — all read 2026-11-20
- Status: accepted 2026-11-20; the admission stands, no ADR, `docs/norm/roles.md` unchanged
- Basis: `docs/norm/roles.md` "Optional, admitted by trigger" (the Architecture row and the roles each page is tested against), "Five discriminations" 1, `docs/norm/roles.md` "Role boundaries" "Structure / Architecture", and `rules.md` R13 — a page's content decides, not its title or its directory. The reader test decides contracts against architecture: a consumer can file a bug because the page's fact changed (contracts), or the page is read to understand the system (architecture).
- Measure: pages, not characters. A page counts once; a page whose sections serve different jobs carries one row naming what each section becomes.

Fifteen candidate pages across three repositories, plus one repository with no design page. Five pages hold an architecture job: the boundary between a sub-project and the shared package, two bodies of measured platform behaviour, the source-to-platform boundary, and a Python monorepo's default assumptions. Ten are refused by the admission and taken by a neighbouring role — structure for layout and placement, contracts for a surface a consumer relies on, knowledge guides for methods and policies, instruction for commands and constraints. No page is homeless, and personal-agent-kit has no design page at all because its trigger never fired.

The narrowed cut W03 left behind survives the test. The design system stays out: none of the four repositories keeps a visual language as a design page, and every page that states a promise its author must keep is taken by contracts rather than architecture.

## Notes

- A page that serves two jobs is a split candidate for its own repository, not a sign the admission is wrong. Two rows record one: `python-toolkit` `docs/architecture.md` keeps one admitted section inside a charter-and-structure page, and `bun-toolkit` `docs/bun.md` states both invariants and traps. Neither repository is this package's target, so the map records the mix and leaves the split to their own migrations.
- The admission travels further than the home path. Two of the five admitted jobs sit outside a `docs/architecture/` shelf — `bun-toolkit` keeps one in `AGENTS.md`, `gearset` keeps two as `docs/` pages. ADR-0022's path convention is about where a page goes once the role is declared, so this is a property of the samples, not a finding against the admission.
- personal-agent-kit is the clean case of the optional role: no design page because no change needs one, which is what "admitted by trigger" is supposed to look like. Its absence is not evidence that the admission is too narrow.
