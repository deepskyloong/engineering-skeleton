# W14 — Give evidence one entry per artifact

**Depends on**: none

The Evidence field had no shape rule, and the package showed it: 25 of 27 items carried one paragraph, `W07e` 1,307 non-whitespace holding four commits and two rulings, and `W06` 2,060 characters with six bullets because one bullet held fourteen page measurements. The maintainer reported the paragraph shape on 2026-11-20, after the description form was settled in W13.

ADR-0034 sets the shape: one entry per artifact, naming what it proves — bullets when several, a table when one artifact carries several facts, a sentence when there is one; detail stays in the artifact, cited by path and one clause. `docs/norm/flow.md` "Work items" states it and the template's placeholder names it.

**Done when**:

- the norm gives evidence the entry shape
- the template placeholder matches it
- ADR-0034 is accepted and indexed
- the items in this package are reflowed into the entry shape
- the gate is green
