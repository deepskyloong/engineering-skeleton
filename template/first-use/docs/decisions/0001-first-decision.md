# 0001 — First decision

Status: Accepted

## Context

What forced a choice. Two or three sentences; link the effort or the incident that raised it.

## Decision

What was chosen, in one sentence, stated as a rule a future maintainer can follow.

## Options considered

1. **Option A** — chosen; why it won.
2. **Option B** — rejected; why it lost.
3. **Option C** — rejected; why it lost.

At least one rejected alternative is required. An ADR without one is a note.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier.
- What becomes harder or impossible.
- What must be checked later, and when.
